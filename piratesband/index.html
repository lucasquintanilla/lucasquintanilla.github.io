About

About the band

UsPirates is an upbeat music group led by the music composer Inês Matos, founded in February 2026.

With 14 musicians, this band makes it all possible because they share a strong friendship and a love for music and art. They sing in different languages and styles, representing their multicultural background.

With its own visual identity, UsPirates transmit an outstanding personality within Dublin's music, dance and art scene. The band is now working on original repertoire and aiming to release its first EP in 2027.

Open to collaboration

UsPirates is open to collaboration with artists such as solo musicians, bands, dancers, visual artists, as well as venues and organisations.

Bio

Our story

UsPirates started in a café in Blackrock in February 2026, when Lucas and Inês came up with the idea of combining their musical knowledge in a project where they could explore the artistic talents of their friends to create music and make people dance.

What began as just an idea has grown into a group of musicians, each bringing their own skills and personality to the project. One of the things that makes UsPirates unique is the combination of a choir with brass and percussion, creating a bigger, richer sound.

With live performance in mind, UsPirates rehearsals had a designated clothing style by Giulia Diano, and they invited photographer friends Arezo, Sofia and Alex to capture every session. This artistic vision aimed to make the group feel like a united crew and be stage-ready. These rehearsals were led by Inês Matos, who organised and structured them, giving a clear musical direction.

In July 2026, UsPirates performed for the first time at a Latin concert at Bello Bar in Dublin city, sharing the stage with Latin artists like Saturno and Tumbao Dublin.

Now, UsPirates are turning their attention to creating their own material, working on their first original songs with the aim of releasing them as an EP by 2027.
